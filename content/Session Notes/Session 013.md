Moss - notices that everyone has kind of spread out to do their own thing. Follows their lead by spreading out to a corner. Just going to drink and enjoy a day off. Sit with her own thoughts, something she doesn’t do much anymore. The bar noise moves to the background. Gets a strange feeling, i do just have my thoughts here. Feels like she’s missing something.

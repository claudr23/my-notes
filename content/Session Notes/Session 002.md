Ayana: I see a few elves of the retinue slink away, they’re back by the bleachers 3-4 elves, opposite direction of castle 14 insight I see them turn and they get lost in the crowd. King points at me and people in the crowd while talking to the bishop 7 perception I hear “question” and “runners” Bishop with knights are approaching me

  

I point “they’re being suspicious, they had something to do with it, give me leave to follow” 12 persuasion bishop - “take them in, if they run, arrest them. If they don’t run, bring them to me”

  

Colum is unprepared for this political theater, puts himself in between me and the bishop with 3 guards, wants to question the retinue of celethorn, not under arrest unless you run.

Bishop does holy magic, casts spell 15 wisdom saving throw I recognize as zone of truth, the caster will know if I try to resist 6 charisma saving throw feeble attempt at trying to resist. Can feel tongue begin to betray my will, cannot speak a deliberate lie within circle

Don’t know what agent it was: yes

Free to go, highly advise caution

“I need to meet my friends and make sure they aren’t judged too harshly, I will meet you back at the cart”

Any attempts to heal him of this odd ailment have come up short, even from the grand bishop of the sun cathedral. From the King: a reward has been placed to any that can find a way to awaken the prince, whoever finds a way will be granted with land, a castle, a barony in Heath. For the prince and the royal family.” knights start running back to their camps to set off on the quest

  

Ayana grabs Emmeline's arm and start pushing through the crowd to find out what’s happened to Celethorn, the others follow us and we walk up to the gate.

  

(livery from earlier loyal to magnorum, gray eagle on black background)

Even the lord Magister came through but he’s still there. Whatever is afflicting him isn’t magical by nature. The prisoner was brought down to the dungeons.”

  

Would Emmeline know that this could lead to war? There hasn’t been for thousands of years but there are tensions at the isthmus. Delicate political situation. The royal family cannot do nothing in this situation (they’ve arrested Celethorn, that’s what they can do)

  

“They’re not letting anyone into the castle unless the king says so”
